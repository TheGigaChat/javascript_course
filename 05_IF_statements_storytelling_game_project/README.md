## Dialogues from the Game

### Branch - `A Young Cook`

Prison officer - Listen up, rookie. Welcome to our yard. You gotta choose your path wisely, or our boys will boot you out. Time's short, so I'll only show you one room—Kitchen or Gym, it's your call.

Action -  Go to the kitchen. Prison guard - In here, you'll be cookin' up grub for the whole block. If you wanna earn some street cred, you better put in the work.

Action - Talk to a man. Kyle - I'm Kyle, the head cook around here. Show me what you got with that tomato—prove you can be of use.

Action - Slice the tomato just like I asked.

Kyle -  That cut was one of the roughest I've seen, but I'll let you stick around in the kitchen. Achievement - A Young Cook

### Branch - ` Exile Slime`

### Branch - `Arrested Hooligan`

Prison officer - Listen up, rookie. Welcome to our yard. You gotta choose your path wisely, or our boys will boot you out. Time's short, so I'll only show you one room—Kitchen or Gym, it's your call.

Action -  Go to the kitchen. Prison guard - In here, you'll be cookin' up grub for the whole block. If you wanna earn some street cred, you better put in the work.

Action - Chow down on a bowl of ramen.
That ramen turned out surprisingly solid for this joint.

Action - Kyle storms in, his face set and ready for trouble. Kyle - Yo, rookie, what’s the deal? You swiped my ramen?

Action - You launch a powerful kick with your strong legs.

Kyle crumples to the floor, knocked out cold. The guards spot your move and cuff you—welcome to your first arrest.
Achievement - Arrested Hooligan
